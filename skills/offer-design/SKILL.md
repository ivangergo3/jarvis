# Offer design

## Inputs

Audience/problem evidence, available deliverable, capacity, cost assumptions and any approved price/terms.

## Procedure

Define what the buyer receives, who benefits, exclusions, delivery conditions and the next decision. Separate the product promise from a service obligation and avoid accidental unlimited commitments.
Compare a small number of offer structures against actual capacity, customer risk and contribution assumptions. Treat proposed prices and guarantees as hypotheses; explain missing cost or willingness-to-pay evidence.
Make trial duration, renewal, eligibility and cancellation terms legible when supplied. Identify proof needed and a bounded validation approach. Do not invent urgency or scarcity to force a purchase.

## Output

An offer brief with value, scope, terms, assumptions, proof needs and a validation question.

## Boundary

Does not set live prices, grant refunds, make legal promises or commit delivery capacity. Unknown terms remain open.
