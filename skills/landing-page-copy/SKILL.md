# Landing page copy

## Inputs

Audience, offer, current capabilities, approved claims/evidence, page purpose and tone.

## Procedure

Choose one primary reader and action. Organize the page around the problem, relevant benefit, how it works, proof, limits and next step in an order suited to the decision.
Tie factual claims to supplied evidence. Distinguish proposed wording from confirmed terms, use explicit placeholders for missing consequential facts and keep placeholders out of publication-ready status.
Make costs, trial terms and material restrictions visible. Match the headline promise to the actual product and next page. Use readable headings and useful links; do not replace evidence with superlatives or deceptive comparison claims.

## Output

Sectioned page copy with CTA, evidence/term gaps and brief rationale outside the copy.

## Boundary

No website edits, publication, invented testimonials or unapproved guarantees. This skill writes copy; it does not implement a page.
