# Work prioritisation

## Inputs

The objective, candidate work, current commitments, constraints, and available evidence of value and effort.

## Procedure

Check each item contributes to the objective. Identify prerequisites and separate urgent defects from discretionary improvements.
Compare expected benefit, risk reduction, effort, confidence, and cost of delay. Use ranges or qualitative estimates when data is weak; do not fabricate commercial impact.
Propose an actionable next slice and deferred items with reasons. Record assumptions and signals that would trigger reprioritisation.

## Output

An ordered backlog with rationale, dependencies, uncertainty, and a recommended next slice.

## Boundary

Does not authorize all backlog items, start tasks, or define permanent departments.
