# Paid campaign planning

## Inputs

Audience/problem, verified offer, channel constraints, economic assumptions, budget proposal and measurement readiness.

## Procedure

Define the business outcome and qualifying conversion before choosing a channel or creative. State audience rationale without inferring sensitive personal traits. Compare a small viable test with doing no paid acquisition yet.
Propose creative angles and landing-page continuity from supported claims. Show total spend cap, duration, review points and stop conditions as proposals unless already authorized. Budget availability alone is not launch permission.
Identify measurement gaps, contribution assumptions and policy checks needed before execution. Consult current primary platform rules when making channel-specific recommendations; avoid inherited benchmarks or universal scale/kill thresholds.

## Output

Campaign brief with hypotheses, draft creative direction, bounded budget, measurement dependencies and go/no-go gaps.

## Boundary

No ad-account writes, audience uploads, live spending or automated scaling. Do not promise acquisition cost or return.
