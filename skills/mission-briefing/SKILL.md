# Mission briefing

## Inputs

The request, intended beneficiary, available project context, constraints, and decisions already agreed.

## Procedure

Identify the desired change for the user or business and why it matters. Separate the outcome from a suggested implementation. Preserve explicit implementation choices rather than reopening them without reason.
Capture in-scope work, exclusions, deliverables, constraints, and authority already granted. Surface only uncertainties that could materially change the work; make reversible assumptions explicit and continue independent work.
Separate an agreed requirement from a proposal. Record who decides unresolved product, technical, or commercial questions. Do not require repeated approval for an agreed brief.

## Output

A concise brief with outcome, scope, deliverables, constraints, existing authority, assumptions, open decisions, and completion intent.

## Boundary

Does not choose the architecture, allocate agents, write implementation, or send instructions. Acceptance criteria can be developed separately.

## Composition and authority

This skill is a portable capability, not an agent role. One agent may combine it with other relevant skills. Its output can be used by a person or another run; no specific provider, framework, or sibling skill is required. Applying it does not expand the user’s authority grant.
