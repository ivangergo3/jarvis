# Paid campaign analysis

## Inputs

Spend, clicks/outcomes/revenue, reporting windows, attribution rules, refunds/cost scope and campaign changes.

## Procedure

Validate units, dates, conversion definitions, completeness and lag. Calculate supported ratios with counts and denominators; distinguish media CPA from fully loaded CAC and attributed ROAS from profit.
Reconcile overlapping channels and first-party outcomes before aggregating. Separate descriptive patterns from causal effects; creative selection by a delivery algorithm is not a randomized experiment.
Recommend hold, investigate or a bounded next experiment based on evidence and downside. Explain economics in plain language where useful. No data means no verdict, not automatic campaign failure.

## Output

Results table, calculations/assumptions, attribution gaps and a bounded recommendation.

## Boundary

No live pauses, bid changes or scaling. Do not derive reliable LTV from a short campaign or sum duplicate conversions.
