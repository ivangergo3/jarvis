# Customer interview planning

## Inputs

Research decision, target participants, current hypotheses, known evidence and interview constraints.

## Procedure

Translate the decision into questions about recent concrete behaviour, workarounds, consequences and alternatives. Separate discovering a problem from pitching a solution or asking whether someone likes an idea.
Choose participants for the question and name recruitment bias. Sequence a short guide with neutral prompts and follow-ups for specific examples. Include disconfirming evidence and avoid treating hypothetical willingness to pay as a purchase.
Plan consent, recording choices and minimal retention of identifiable details. Define what evidence would update the hypothesis and what a small qualitative sample cannot establish.

## Output

Learning objectives, participant criteria, neutral interview guide, capture/consent plan and decision limits.

## Boundary

No recruitment messages, recording, scheduling or contacting participants from a planning request.
