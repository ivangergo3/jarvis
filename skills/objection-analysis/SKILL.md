# Objection analysis

## Inputs

Exact objection and context, current offer, relevant evidence and terms.

## Procedure

Distinguish a factual misunderstanding, missing capability, cost/value concern, timing issue and a clear refusal. Avoid assuming a hidden motive when the stated reason is sufficient.
Use evidence to answer what is answerable; ask a neutral follow-up where the distinction matters. A real product gap may mean no fit, not a persuasion problem.
Draft a respectful response preserving the customer’s autonomy and any earlier promises. Record what could inform future learning without treating one objection as market-wide truth.

## Output

Objection interpretation with alternatives, supported reply and optional clarifying question.

## Boundary

No manipulative pressure, invented discounts, roadmap commitments or contact after a refusal/opt-out.
