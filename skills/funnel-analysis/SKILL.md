# Funnel analysis

## Inputs

Stage definitions, event/count data, identity rules, time window/cohort and known collection gaps.

## Procedure

Check whether counts represent people, accounts, sessions or events, and whether stages share the same eligible cohort and window. Repeated events and late conversions can make naïve ratios misleading.
Calculate stage conversion and overall progression only when denominators match. Show counts beside rates and distinguish absolute percentage-point changes from relative changes. Segment only where data and sample size support it.
Identify the largest supported drop-offs, instrumentation questions and hypotheses. A rate change alone does not establish the cause or financial value. Suggest the next evidence check before a redesign.

## Output

Funnel table with counts/rates, data validity notes, hypotheses and next checks.

## Boundary

No tracking installation or raw personal-data upload. Do not divide incompatible event/cohort counts into a conversion rate.
