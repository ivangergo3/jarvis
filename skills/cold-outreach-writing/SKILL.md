# Cold outreach writing

## Inputs

Recipient/account relevance, truthful sender context, offer, available proof and requested next action.

## Procedure

Use a verified business observation only if it explains relevance; avoid guessed personal facts or fabricated familiarity. State the sender’s reason for writing transparently.
Connect a bounded benefit to the possible problem without claiming the recipient has it. Keep one reasonable next step and remove unsupported social proof, false urgency and deceptive reply-style subjects.
Prepare a draft with evidence gaps outside the copy. If asked about sending readiness, identify eligibility, suppression, recipient jurisdiction and provider rules to verify from current authoritative sources; do not turn generic copy advice into a legal conclusion or a universal sending cadence.

## Output

Subject and message draft, source of personalization, claim gaps and applicable send-readiness unknowns.

## Boundary

Drafting does not send, schedule, buy lists, register domains or authorize contact. Opt-outs and objections are not bypassed by another channel.
