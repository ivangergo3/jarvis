# Follow up writing

## Inputs

Previous messages, recipient response/status, elapsed time, current offer and permitted communication scope.

## Procedure

Read the actual history and distinguish no response, a question, an objection, an explicit later date and an opt-out. A reply changes the workflow; do not blindly continue a prepared sequence.
Add useful new information or answer the recipient’s question. Preserve promises made earlier without creating new ones. If proposing cadence, use the request and context rather than a universal number of touches.
State stop conditions and missing information outside the draft. If the prior send outcome is uncertain, reconcile it before planning another send; do not claim delivery from a local draft.

## Output

A relevant reply/follow-up draft or a reason to stop, with sequence state and next check.

## Boundary

No sending, scheduling, cross-channel pursuit after opt-out or automatic resend. Do not invent prior conversations.
