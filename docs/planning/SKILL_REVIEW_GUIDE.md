# Consolidated skill review

This change implements the agreed skill definitions, not the app or an agent team. Start with the [68-skill catalogue](SKILL_CATALOG.md); the [backlog](SKILL_BACKLOG.md) groups it into the existing tickets.

## Suggested reading order

1. [Boundaries](SKILL_BOUNDARIES.md) and [local-use safety](SKILL_SAFETY.md): portability, authority, private context and runtime limits.
2. [Research notes](SKILL_RESEARCH.md): external inspiration and what was deliberately excluded.
3. [Evaluation report](../../evaluations/library-2026-11-15/REPORT.md): actual outcomes, revisions and limitations. Raw prompts, outputs and review evidence are alongside it.
4. Focused skills in whichever family you want to assess. Each has a short procedure and normal/boundary exercises. No sibling skill is mandatory.

## Decisions to review

- Are the triggers and outputs useful independently, with enough separation to compose them freely?
- Do the engineering skills preserve accepted requirements and produce evidence rather than assume success?
- Do commercial skills explain the evidence and economics, preserve truthful claims and distinguish drafting from execution?
- Are data, authority and uncertain-side-effect boundaries clear without forcing repeated approval for work already authorized?
- Are any definitions over-specific, overlapping or missing a capability from the agreed scope?

The current board uses Backlog, In progress, In review, Blocked and Done. Definition tickets stay open until acceptance and merge; a passing synthetic exercise is evidence for review, not automatic acceptance. Future live assignments may expose additional improvements, and those should retain their failures and version history.

Personal adaptations are maintained separately from this public change. Review their private files in their own repository; no private source material or identifying links are included here.
