# Prioritised skill backlog

Status: 15 November 2026. All agreed families now have focused definitions. Consolidated review is in PR #42; current evaluation outcomes and limitations are in the [library report](../../evaluations/library-2026-11-15/REPORT.md). The original [P0 report](../../evaluations/p0-2026-11-08/REPORT.md) remains historical evidence for its exact versions.

P0 means foundational reasoning and evidence; P1 means first engineering/content/sales workflows; P2 means broader acquisition, operations and skill maintenance. Priority was authoring order, not a limit on availability or permission to execute. There are 68 portable skills. Family IDs group related capabilities without prescribing roles. See the [full catalogue](SKILL_CATALOG.md) for each trigger and source file.

## Review and placement

The existing 38 cards remain readable family/foundation deliverables; focused child skills are listed on their family ticket. Splitting them into separate cards can wait until they generate independently scheduled work. Do not mark a family Done until its acceptance and required merge are recorded.

Generic personal-brand capabilities are public. Any author-specific voice, biography or private references belong in local/private adaptations and are not dependencies of this library. Project-specific rules belong in the applicable AGENTS.md.

No app implementation, lasting team/provider setup, global installation or live commercial execution is included. See [research provenance](SKILL_RESEARCH.md), [evaluation method](SKILL_EVALUATION.md) and [board guide](BOARD_GUIDE.md).
