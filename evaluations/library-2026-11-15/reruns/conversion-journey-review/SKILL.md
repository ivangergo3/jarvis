# Conversion journey review

## Inputs

Journey surfaces or screenshots, intended audience/action, offer terms and any measured funnel data.

## Procedure

Trace the intended path and what each step asks the user to believe or do. Compare promises, pricing, eligibility and action labels across surfaces. Identify concrete mismatches and avoid calling every hesitation a conversion defect.
Distinguish usability/accessibility observations from performance hypotheses. Attribute each observation to its actual source: a supplied screenshot or description is not a journey you executed. Use direct-observation language only for checks actually performed. Prioritize barriers by evidence and customer consequence; suggest a focused change or test with a relevant outcome.
Respect informed choice: cancellation, consent and material terms should stay clear. An increase in clicks is not success if it produces unwanted purchases, support load or misleading expectations.

## Output

Journey findings with evidence, proposed improvements, testable hypotheses and measurement gaps.

## Boundary

No live design edits, funnel configuration or dark patterns. Screenshots alone cannot prove a conversion uplift.
