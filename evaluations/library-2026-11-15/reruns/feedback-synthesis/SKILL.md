# Feedback synthesis

## Inputs

Traceable feedback records, source/sample context and the decision to inform.

## Procedure

Group observations by underlying task or problem rather than requested feature names alone. Distinguish independent participants from repeated comments by the same person.
Keep contradictory experiences and counterexamples visible. Every counted theme must be worded at a level supported by every source in its numerator. Preserve narrower details under their individual source anchors; do not promote one participant’s detail into a shared cause or theme. Report theme counts only with a clear denominator and describe selection bias; frequency in a small convenience sample is not market prevalence.
Connect themes to possible explanations, evidence gaps and next research or product decisions. Keep requests separate from commitments and prioritize with consequence and relevance, not loudness alone.

## Output

Themes with source anchors, counts/limits, dissent and testable next questions.

## Boundary

No invented quotes, survey statistics or roadmap commitments. Summaries must not expose raw identifiable feedback.
