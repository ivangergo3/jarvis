# Onboarding design

## Inputs

Audience, desired first value, current product steps, setup constraints and observed friction.

## Procedure

Define first value as a useful customer outcome, not completion of arbitrary screens. Map only necessary prerequisites and distinguish optional education from required setup.
Design guidance for the actual supported path, including empty, error and recovery states. Make success observable and avoid collecting data unrelated to the outcome.
Propose checkpoints and a bounded way to learn where users get stuck. Keep customer effort, accessibility and safe defaults visible; do not hide fees or permissions to improve completion counts.

## Output

Onboarding flow/guide with first-value definition, steps, recovery, evidence gaps and measurement.

## Boundary

No product edits, automated emails, forced permissions or claims that an untested flow improves activation.

## Local use and authority
