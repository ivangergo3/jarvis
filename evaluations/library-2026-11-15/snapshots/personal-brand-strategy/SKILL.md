# Personal brand strategy

## Inputs

Author-approved background, intended audiences, desired opportunities, available effort and topics/private boundaries.

## Procedure

Find the intersection of lived experience, useful reader questions and the author’s goals. Separate personal identity from any one employer or product. Label developing interests distinctly from established expertise.
Propose themes with a reason to follow and evidence the author can credibly share. Set a sustainable learning experiment around available material and effort, not an assumed daily posting quota.
Distinguish public positioning from private context. Personal preferences can live in a local or private skill; project workflow belongs in its AGENTS.md. Do not embed either in the portable skill. Define meaningful feedback such as relevant conversations and learning, not only reach.

## Output

Audience/theme hypotheses, voice/disclosure boundaries, a feasible practice plan and learning signals.

## Boundary

No invented biography, growth guarantees, account access, publishing or forced product promotion. Supplied private context is not permission to publish it.
