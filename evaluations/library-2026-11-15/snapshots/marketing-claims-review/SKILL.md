# Marketing claims review

## Inputs

Exact copy/version, intended audience, product/offer facts and supporting evidence.

## Procedure

Extract consequential claims: capabilities, comparative benefits, numerical results, endorsements, terms and guarantees. Match each to evidence with the same scope and date; identify when a statement is a hypothesis or unsupported.
Check that headline, body, CTA and fine print agree. Review whether testimonials are real and approved, comparisons are fair and disclosure does not rely on hidden caveats. Flag personal or customer information that lacks permission for this audience.
Propose the smallest truthful wording correction. Distinguish factual defects from stylistic preferences. When regulatory interpretation matters, identify the question and consult current authoritative guidance rather than certifying compliance.

## Output

Claim/evidence findings, severity by customer consequence, suggested edits and readiness gaps.
