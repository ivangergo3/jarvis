# Growth experiment design

## Inputs

Hypothesis, target population, baseline/data quality, change, constraints and risk/budget scope.

## Procedure

Specify treatment, comparison, assignment unit and exposure/analysis population. Choose the primary outcome, guardrails and follow-up window before observing results. Explain possible interference, novelty and selection effects.
Use a sample-size or duration rationale based on baseline and the smallest effect worth acting on; if inputs are missing, name them instead of inventing statistical power. Plan assignment and tracking checks.
Set a stopping/analysis rule and treatment of multiple comparisons or repeated peeking. Distinguish an exploratory pilot from a confirmatory test. Define cleanup and a reversible next step; customer choice and valid consent remain constraints.

## Output

Experiment brief with hypothesis, design, metrics, feasibility, stopping rule, risks and decision conditions.

## Boundary

Design is not launch, spending or experimentation on users. Do not guarantee uplift or force a test where traffic cannot answer the question.
