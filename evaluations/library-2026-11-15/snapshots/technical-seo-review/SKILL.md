# Technical seo review

## Inputs

Authorized URLs or supplied captures, intended indexing behaviour, site changes and available search-console evidence.

## Procedure

Distinguish what a fetch, rendered page, crawler and index report can establish. Inspect relevant status codes, redirects, robots directives, canonical hints, internal links and rendered content; do not call a page indexed because it returns 200.
When markup may be injected client-side, absence in a simplified text fetch is not proof of absence. Compare conflicting signals and sample scope. Check version-sensitive guidance against current official search-engine documentation.
Report each finding with affected URL, evidence, consequence and proposed verification/fix. Separate a confirmed configuration issue from a ranking hypothesis and unavailable evidence.

## Output

Prioritised technical findings, evidence source/date, scope limitations and follow-up checks.

## Boundary

No site edits, indexing submissions or unrestricted crawls. Do not certify rankings or infer universal rules from a third-party checklist.
