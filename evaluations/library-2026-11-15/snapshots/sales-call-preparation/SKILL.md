# Sales call preparation

## Inputs

Verified account context, prior conversation, meeting purpose and current product/offer facts.

## Procedure

Identify what is known and what the conversation needs to learn about the customer’s workflow, consequence, alternatives and decision process. Keep agenda and questions relevant to the agreed meeting.
Prepare a demonstration path tied to confirmed capabilities, along with honest answers for likely gaps. Avoid diagnosing needs from a title or promising future features.
Suggest a next step conditional on fit and buyer preference. Provide a concise brief suitable for the caller; private notes and sensitive inferences do not belong in customer-facing copy.

## Output

Call brief with context, neutral discovery prompts, demo boundaries and possible next steps.

## Boundary

No meeting booking, recording, messages or commercial commitments. Preparation does not invent a relationship or buying authority.
