# Prospect qualification

## Inputs

Account facts with provenance, qualification criteria, disqualifiers and scoring rules if already agreed.

## Procedure

Evaluate each criterion using evidence of the right granularity and date. Separate organization fit, possible need and demonstrated intent; do not infer authority or consent from a job title.
Use an agreed rubric if supplied; otherwise propose a simple qualitative rubric and label it as proposed. Keep unknown criteria visible rather than converting missing data to zero or certainty.
Recommend the next low-cost evidence check or a reason to deprioritize. Do not force every account into a sales-ready bucket; state what would change the assessment.

## Output

Fit assessment with evidence per criterion, unknowns, disqualifiers and next action.

## Boundary

No autonomous outreach, CRM writes, sensitive-trait scoring or invented purchase intent. Qualification does not establish permission to contact.
