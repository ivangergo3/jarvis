# Seo content planning

## Inputs

Intent/topic research, audience, existing pages, source material and current product facts.

## Procedure

Map one coherent reader task to a page or update. Check existing coverage to avoid unnecessary duplication; consolidation or an improved existing page may serve the reader better than new volume.
Specify the answer, evidence/examples needed, outline, relevant internal links and useful next step. Distinguish editorial suggestions from search-engine requirements. Do not impose arbitrary word counts, keyword density or mass publishing quotas.
Plan original value and factual review. Mark claims and product capabilities needing confirmation. Define how usefulness and appropriate search outcomes will be observed over a meaningful window without guaranteeing placement.

## Output

Content brief or prioritised plan with intent, outline, evidence needs, existing-page decision and measurement.

## Boundary

No copied competitor text, fabricated expertise, doorway-page scaling or automatic publication.
