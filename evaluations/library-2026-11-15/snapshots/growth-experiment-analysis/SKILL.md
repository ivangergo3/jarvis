# Growth experiment analysis

## Inputs

Protocol, assignment/exposure data, outcomes, time window, deviations and measurement quality.

## Procedure

Verify assignment balance, eligibility, missing outcomes, follow-up maturity and guardrails before interpreting lift. If the design is absent or invalid, describe the comparison as observational or exploratory as appropriate.
Report sample sizes, rates, absolute difference and relative change with compatible denominators. Use a statistical method suited to the design and report uncertainty; do not invent confidence intervals or significance from intuition.
Judge the result against the stated decision threshold and costs, not positivity alone. Preserve null/negative findings and protocol deviations. Recommend adoption, further evidence or stopping with an explicit rationale.

## Output

A decision readout with data checks, effects, uncertainty, guardrails, deviations and limits.

## Boundary

No automatic rollout or budget change. An inconclusive result does not prove no effect; an observed association does not prove causality.
