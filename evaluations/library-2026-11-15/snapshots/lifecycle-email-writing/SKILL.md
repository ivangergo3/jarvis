# Lifecycle email writing

## Inputs

Lifecycle event, audience eligibility, current product/offer facts, desired action and approved tone.

## Procedure

Connect the message to the recipient’s actual stage: onboarding, use, renewal or a known service event. Explain why it matters and offer one relevant next action.
Distinguish service information from promotional content. State assumed eligibility and suppression conditions; do not infer consent from possession of an address. Verify time-sensitive dates, links and terms before calling a draft ready.
Keep subjects accurate and avoid implying an action or payment already happened without evidence. For a sequence, specify exit conditions such as completion or opt-out so drafts do not assume continued eligibility.

## Output

Subject, preview and body drafts with trigger/eligibility, stop conditions and unresolved facts.

## Boundary

No email sends, scheduling, contact uploads or legal-compliance certification. Any future send needs applicable authority and current eligibility checks.
