# Metric definition

## Inputs

The decision, business model, available data, reporting period, and proposed metric.

## Procedure

Define numerator, denominator, units, period or cohort, inclusions, exclusions, and attribution basis. Separate paying customers, trials, users, accounts, revenue, and profit.
For CAC, distinguish paid-channel acquisition cost from fully loaded acquisition cost. For retention and churn, specify customer versus revenue measures and the cohort/window.
Describe required data and known gaps. Use a short concrete example when the user is learning; do not impose a universal healthy benchmark.

## Output

A metric definition or measurement contract with formula, data needs, example, assumptions, and interpretation limits.

## Boundary

Does not silently change definitions to improve reported performance or treat an estimate as an observed metric.
