# Unit economics analysis

## Inputs

Metric definitions, revenue and cost data, period/cohorts, retention evidence, and assumptions.

## Procedure

Check definitions and time bases are compatible. Separate observed economics from scenarios; distinguish revenue-based lifetime value from gross-profit contribution.
Choose a model justified by the business and evidence. A constant-churn recurring-revenue approximation requires stable assumptions and sufficient history; explain when early or changing cohorts make it unreliable.
Show calculations, sensitivity to consequential assumptions, and limits. When lifetime value cannot be estimated credibly, report observed cohort contribution or a labelled scenario and suggest what to measure next.

## Output

An economics assessment with formulas, inputs, units, calculations, scenarios, uncertainty, and decision implications.

## Boundary

Does not promise profitability, fabricate retention, prescribe a universal LTV-to-CAC ratio, or authorize spending.
