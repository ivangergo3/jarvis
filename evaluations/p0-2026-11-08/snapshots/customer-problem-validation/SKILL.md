# Customer problem validation

## Inputs

The proposed problem, intended segment, customer evidence, existing alternatives, and assumptions.

## Procedure

Express the problem as a situation, consequence, current workaround, and reason to change. Separate a requested feature from the underlying problem.
Rank evidence by what it demonstrates: expressed interest, observed behaviour, repeated use, commitment, or payment are different signals. Do not treat friendly feedback as purchase intent.
Identify disconfirming evidence and propose a proportionate research question or experiment with a decision criterion. Keep research execution and participant contact within separately granted scope.

## Output

A problem hypothesis, supporting and conflicting evidence, key assumptions, and a proposed validation step.

## Boundary

Does not invent interviews, claim demand from likes, or automatically send surveys or recruit participants.
