# Decision recording

## Inputs

The decision conversation, relevant evidence, alternatives, and existing decision record if present.

## Procedure

Identify whether a decision was actually made. Record the decider, date, scope, chosen option, rationale, consequential alternatives, and follow-up where known.
Separate unresolved questions and reversible assumptions from approved choices. Record when a later decision supersedes an earlier one and why.
Preserve the user’s existing authority without expanding it to unrelated actions. Save only to the authorized destination and verify persistence when requested.

## Output

A short decision record with status, context, choice, rationale, consequences, and supersession links where applicable.

## Boundary

Does not seek ceremonial approval for an already explicit decision, invent an owner, or turn tentative interest into permission.

## Composition and authority

This skill is a portable capability, not an agent role. One agent may combine it with other relevant skills. Its output can be used by a person or another run; no specific provider, framework, or sibling skill is required. Applying it does not expand the user’s authority grant.
