# Product positioning

## Inputs

Target segment, validated or hypothesised problem, current product capabilities, alternatives, and supporting evidence.

## Procedure

Describe the relevant alternative and the reason the target customer would switch. Connect current capabilities to a concrete benefit without inventing measured outcomes.
State category, audience, problem, distinctive value, and support for the claim. Separate a positioning hypothesis from validated differentiation.
Provide a concise positioning statement, message pillars, claims requiring proof, and a proposed validation question. Keep product positioning distinct from the founder’s broader personal identity.

## Output

A positioning brief with audience, alternative, value, evidence, message pillars, and unresolved claims.

## Boundary

Does not set prices, invent testimonials, promise integrations, or produce a full campaign unless separately requested.
