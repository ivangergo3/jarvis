# Customer segmentation

## Inputs

Product capabilities, candidate markets, known users, research, constraints, and commercial objective.

## Procedure

Separate user, buyer, approver, and beneficiary when they differ. Describe segments by meaningful problems, context, buying constraints, and ability to reach them.
Assess problem urgency, alternatives, plausible buying process, and evidence of willingness to act. Mark hypotheses explicitly; use demographic or firmographic detail only when it changes the decision.
Recommend a segment to investigate and the evidence needed before committing. Keep an individual’s personal-brand audience separate from a product’s sales audience.

## Output

Candidate segments, buyer/user distinction, evidence, unknowns, and a recommended validation focus.

## Boundary

Does not scrape or contact prospects, invent willingness to pay, or assume one product segment defines all personal content.
