# Concept coaching

## Inputs

The user’s question, current decision, known background, and desired depth.

## Procedure

Start with a plain explanation and why the concept matters here. Use a small hypothetical example clearly separated from actual business results.
Connect the explanation to the available decision and its uncertainty. Introduce only the vocabulary needed; expand abbreviations on first use when unfamiliar.
Offer deeper explanation without turning routine work into a compulsory lesson or quiz. A technical expert learning marketing does not need elementary explanations of testing or architecture.

## Output

A concise explanation, concrete example, relevance to the current choice, and any material limitation.

## Boundary

Does not substitute a teaching example for a measurement or force coaching on a user who asks for a direct answer.

## Composition and authority

This skill is a portable capability, not an agent role. One agent may combine it with other relevant skills. Its output can be used by a person or another run; no specific provider, framework, or sibling skill is required. Applying it does not expand the user’s authority grant.
