## Normal task output

**Positioning hypothesis:** For small agencies coordinating monthly timesheets through spreadsheets and email, this is a timesheet submission and approval tool that gives staff a submission workflow and managers an approve-or-return decision, with the decision visible to staff.

- Audience: agency owners responsible for the process, managers reviewing submissions, and staff submitting hours. The buying decision and budget remain to be confirmed.
- Category: monthly timesheet submission and approval software.
- Alternative: spreadsheet tracking, email submissions, and manual follow-up.
- Problem: three interviewed owners describe chasing submissions. The severity and wider prevalence remain uncertain.
- Value: a defined route from submission to review and a visible outcome. The reason to switch is the possibility of making this process easier to coordinate; reduced chasing has not been demonstrated.
- Evidence: current capabilities support submission, manager approve/return, and visible decisions. Three interviews support the problem hypothesis; no owner has committed to buy. There is no evidence of unique competitive superiority.

Message pillars:

1. Staff submit monthly timesheets through a defined workflow.
2. Managers approve or return submissions for further attention.
3. Staff can see the review decision.

Validate reduced chasing, adoption effort, willingness to switch/pay, and whether this differs meaningfully from their current options. Do not claim measured savings, payroll automation, SSO, testimonials, or proven demand.

Proposed validation question: “Looking at your last monthly cycle, where would this workflow change what you did, and what would stop you using it next month?” Follow with a request for a bounded real-cycle pilot if the fit is credible.

## Boundary task output

**Positioning hypothesis:** For agency owners keeping track of team administration, this is a tool for tracking hours, leave requests, and staff files in one place.

Category: team administration software. The proposed alternative is maintaining those records across separate files and tools; confirm that this reflects the target agency's actual workflow. The proposed benefit is a single place to manage these administrative records. How much easier this is, and whether it motivates switching, remain unvalidated.

Message pillars supported by current capability:

1. Track staff hours.
2. Keep track of leave requests.
3. Maintain staff files alongside team administration records.

Replace the sales draft's payroll automation, guaranteed compliance, and “10 hours weekly” statements with these capability-based messages. No payroll integration, legal assessment, or time-savings measurement supports those claims. Avoid claiming approval workflows or other capabilities not verified in this brief.

Ask agency owners to describe their current hours, leave, and staff-record process, identify the most costly recurring difficulty, and walk through whether the current tool addresses it. Validate switching motivation, missing requirements, and actual time outcomes before strengthening the positioning. No price, testimonial, or campaign is created here.
