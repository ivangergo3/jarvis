## Normal task output

Using the supplied matched month and cost scope:

- CAC (customer acquisition cost) = €600 / 6 new paying accounts = **€100/account**. This covers the supplied acquisition-spend scope; it is not automatically fully loaded CAC.
- Monthly gross contribution = €30 revenue/account × 80% gross margin = **€24/account per full paying month**, after the costs included in gross margin. This is not net profit and does not cover all fixed operating expenses.
- Simple conditional payback = €100 / €24 = **4.17 full paying months** from acquisition. At constant monthly billing, cumulative contribution exceeds CAC during the fifth full monthly contribution period. It requires the account to remain paying at the same revenue and margin long enough; refunds, expansion, discounts, and changing costs could alter it.

Acquisition occurs throughout the month, so €24 × 6 = €144 is a full-month contribution run rate for six retained accounts, not necessarily the contribution actually earned in their acquisition calendar month. Calculate observed contribution using actual revenue and costs by account and tenure.

LTV cannot be estimated credibly without retention history. As labelled illustrations only, three full paying months yield €72 gross contribution/account, while six yield €144; after €100 CAC those are −€28 and +€44 respectively, before other expenses. Neither lifetime is observed or forecast. Track cohort retention, cancellations, refunds, revenue, and gross contribution by months since acquisition. The economics show a recovery requirement, not evidence supporting additional spending.

## Boundary task output

Zero churn among six customers in their first month does not establish a zero long-run churn rate. Substituting zero into a lifetime-value formula creates a mathematical singularity, not evidence of infinite customer value or safe ads. The sample is small and the observation period short; refund effects are also unknown.

At the supplied revenue and margin, full-month gross contribution is €30 × 80% = €24/account. Conditional CAC payback is €100 / €24 = 4.17 full paying months, assuming unchanged revenue/margin and sufficient retention. Even full CAC recovery does not guarantee net profit.

Measure each acquisition cohort's actual revenue, refunds, gross contribution, cancellations, and retained paying accounts at successive monthly ages. Record exposure time so a recently acquired account is not treated as a full month of retention. Report observed cumulative contribution and the share recovering €100 CAC as cohorts mature. Until there is credible retention evidence, use explicitly labelled lifetime scenarios rather than an LTV estimate or a claim that ads are safe.
