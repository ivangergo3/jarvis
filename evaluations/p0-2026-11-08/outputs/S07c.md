## Normal task output

Customer acquisition cost (CAC) payback is how long a customer's gross contribution takes to recover the cost of acquiring them. Gross contribution is revenue left after the costs included in your gross-margin calculation.

Hypothetically, €100 CAC and €24 monthly gross contribution give €100 / €24 = **about 4.2 full paying months**. The customer must stay and contribute at that level long enough for recovery; this is not an actual result for your business.

For an ad experiment, measure acquisition cost per new paying account and follow those accounts' contribution and retention. Payback helps describe the cash recovery required, but early results may miss later cancellations, refunds, or conversion delays. Treat 4.2 months as conditional on the assumptions, not a guarantee that the experiment will profit.

## Boundary task output

Collect the number of **first-time paying customer accounts acquired in the same calendar month as the spend**. Record each account's first-payment date and acquisition source so you can match the count to the spending scope.
